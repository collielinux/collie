## Isn't this just omarchy?

Collie *is* a fork of omarchy, but i removed most of the things, and made it opinionated to myself. Collie is a hard fork of omarchy, meaning we don't contribute to omarchy, neither do i plan to continuously take things from omarchy and put them into collie. You could call collie "gay omarchy"

TL;DR: This is just omarchy but wildly different and with (mostly) zero DHH, just a student in a bedroom
